IoT Flood Alert System

Developed a real time flood detection system using sensors and GSM modules.

This project presents a smart IoT-based flood detection and early warning system designed to improve community safety in flood-prone areas. It integrates an Arduino Uno, ESP32, ultrasonic sensor, and GSM module to monitor water levels and deliver real-time alerts.

The system combines live sensor data with weather forecast information retrieved via the ESP32, enabling early prediction of potential flood events. When critical thresholds are reached, alerts are sent to users, allowing timely evacuation and risk mitigation.

By leveraging real-time data processing and IoT communication, this solution enhances disaster preparedness and helps reduce property damage and loss of life.

Solar Cell Characteristics

Photovoltaic Modeling & MATLAB/Simulink

Analyzed the V-I and P-V characteristics of a solar cell under varying irradiance and temperature conditions using MATLAB and Simulink.

This project investigated the electrical characteristics of a photovoltaic solar cell by studying the relationship between voltage, current, and power output.

A PV array model was developed in MATLAB/Simulink using the required photovoltaic, measurement, switching, control, and display blocks. The simulation was used to obtain current, voltage, and power measurements and generate the corresponding I-V and P-V characteristic curves.

The effect of irradiance was investigated at a constant temperature of 25°C using irradiance levels of 1000, 800, 600, 400, and 200 W/m².

The effect of temperature was also investigated at a constant irradiance of 1000 W/m² using temperatures of 25, 30, 35, 40, and 50°C.

The analysis focused on the effects of irradiance and temperature on the short-circuit current, open-circuit voltage, maximum power, and overall solar-cell performance.

Key Areas:

  • PV solar cell modeling
  • I-V characteristic curves
  • P-V characteristic curves
  • MATLAB/Simulink simulation
  • Variable irradiance analysis
  • Temperature effect analysis
  • Maximum Power Point (MPP)
  • PV efficiency and fill factor

Coupled Tank Control System

Control Engineering & MATLAB/Simulink

Modeled, simulated, linearized, and controlled a coupled two-tank liquid-level system using MATLAB and Simulink.

This project focused on the modeling and analysis of a coupled two-tank liquid-level system. The nonlinear mathematical model was derived using mass balance equations for both tanks, taking into account the inflow and outflow rates, tank areas, orifice areas, gravity, and pump inputs.

The nonlinear system was implemented in MATLAB/Simulink and simulated under different combinations of inflow and outflow parameters. The simulations were used to investigate how the liquid levels in the two tanks change over time and reach steady-state conditions.

The system was then linearized around an equilibrium operating point and represented using a state-space model. The project also involved controller design using pole placement and Linear Quadratic Regulator (LQR) methods.

The controller designs were analyzed based on steady-state performance, transient response, stability, responsiveness, and control effort.

Key Areas:

  • Nonlinear system modeling
  • MATLAB/Simulink simulation
  • Equilibrium point calculation
  • System linearization
  • State-space representation
  • Pole placement control
  • LQR controller design
